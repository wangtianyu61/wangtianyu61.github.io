Research by Topic

My research focuses on the design and deployment of AI-driven decision making in high-stakes environments. Selected papers in three directions (1) trustworthy decisions, (2) AI evaluation, and (3) healthcare operations are included below.

For the papers below, * indicates that authors are listed in alphabetical order. + indicates equal contributions.

Trustworthy decisions

I develop diagnostic tools and trustworthy methods across the data-to-decision pipeline, focusing on often-overlooked yet practically important components, including data acquisition, performance evaluation, and comparison.
